FitChance Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

FitChance predicts your chance of reaching your daily Move, Exercise, and Stand goals on Apple Watch. It was built so your health information stays on your own devices.

The short version

  • Your Health data never leaves your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • FitChance has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • The developer never receives or sees your data.

Information FitChance reads

With your permission, FitChance reads the following from Apple Health:

  • Daily activity (Move, Exercise, and Stand), your goals, and hourly activity
  • Workouts, including workout start locations, which are used only to tell whether you were away from home
  • Sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, wrist temperature, and breathing rate

FitChance never writes anything to Apple Health.

If you choose to turn them on, FitChance also uses:

  • Calendars you select to see how busy your day is (only event times are used)
  • Approximate location to set your home area and tell when you're traveling
  • Your routine, which you type in and which is processed by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone
  • Notifications, which are created on your iPhone

How your information is used

All predictions are calculated on your iPhone. FitChance stores a copy of your activity history on your iPhone so it opens quickly. That copy is protected by your iPhone's passcode.

Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, and it is never sold or shared with anyone.

Information that leaves your device

FitChance makes only two kinds of internet requests, both to Apple:

  1. Weather (optional, can be turned off in Settings → Weather): FitChance sends Apple Weather a rounded location for your home area (about one mile) and a set of dates, and receives weather in return. No Health data is sent.
  2. ZIP code lookup (optional): if you enter a ZIP code to set your home area, it's sent to Apple Maps to find its location.

Apple handles these requests under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Your iPhone and Apple Watch share odds and activity progress directly with each other. This data isn't sent to any server.

Deleting your data

Open FitChance, then go to Settings → Delete My FitChance Data to erase everything FitChance has saved. Deleting the app also removes it. Your data in the Health app isn't affected.

You can turn off FitChance's access at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → FitChance.

Children

FitChance isn't directed to children under 13.
