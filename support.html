Common questions

Why don't I see any odds yet?

FitChance needs 30 days of Apple Watch activity to learn your patterns. Until then, the Today screen shows your progress toward those 30 days. Keep wearing your Watch, and your odds will appear automatically.

FitChance says it can't see my activity data.

Open the Settings app, then go to Health → Data Access & Devices → FitChance and turn on all categories. Then open FitChance and tap Check Again. You'll also need an Apple Watch that tracks your Move, Exercise, and Stand goals.

How are the odds calculated?

FitChance compares today with your own past days at the same time of day, along with factors like your pace, workouts so far, day of week, sleep, recovery, schedule, and weather. It learns entirely on your iPhone, and your data never leaves your devices.

Why did FitChance skip an outdoor workout?

Outdoor suggestions are left out when rain is expected, after dark (except walks and runs), outside your temperature limits, or when it's not a season you usually do that workout. You can set your temperature limits in Settings → Weather → Outdoor workouts.

My activity is paused today.

When you pause your activity goals in the Fitness app, FitChance shows a rest-day message instead of odds and leaves paused days out of its predictions.

Does FitChance work with a Move goal in minutes?

Not yet. FitChance currently supports Move goals measured in calories.

How do I delete my data?

In FitChance, open the Settings menu on the Today screen and choose Delete My FitChance Data. This erases everything FitChance has saved on your iPhone. Your data in the Health app isn't affected.

Why does weather need the internet?

Weather comes from Apple Weather. FitChance sends only a rounded location for your home area and a set of dates. No Health data is ever sent. You can turn weather off in Settings → Weather.
